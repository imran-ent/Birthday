Happy Birthday, my brother.

I wanted to build something way louder and more permanent than a lazy "HBD bro" text at midnight—because real ones like you don't come around twice in a lifetime. Looking at all these photos of us—whether you're making that legendary deadpan face trying to survive a boring family gathering, dodging the camera like you're dodging paparazzi, cruising down ghat roads in a crisp white shirt, or locked into your laptop with Spotify blasting at 3 AM—I realized how many of my best days only happened because you were there.

People throw the word "brother" around way too easily, so let me be dead honest with you: we aren't brothers by blood, we're brothers by choice, and I wouldn't trade that for anything. You have this rare, grounded loyalty where you don't need to make big speeches to prove you care—you just show up. When life gets heavy, when plans fall apart, or when I just need someone who gets the joke without explaining it, you're already in my corner.

Yes, I will roast you relentlessly for blurry hill sprints, dramatic black-and-white selfies, and staring at your phone at the canteen table like a stressed-out CEO. That's literally in my job description as your best friend. But behind every single joke is massive respect for the person you are, the resilience you carry quietly, and the way you stay 100% real in a world full of fake people.

As November 16 kicks off a brand new chapter for you, here is my real wish for you: may every silent goal you're grinding toward right now pay off bigger than you imagined. May your mind stay sharp, your peace stay unbothered, your plate always stay full, and your circle stay solid.

No matter where life takes us next, you've got a day-one brother for life. Enjoy every second of today—the treat is officially on me!

ALWAYS GOT YOUR BACK,
Your Day-One Brother
